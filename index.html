Derive any unit yourself

  1. Write the formula that defines the quantity.
  2. Replace each quantity with its unit.
  3. Simplify. Named units such as N, J and W are shortcuts for long base-unit strings.

Quantities with no unit

  • Refractive index, n = c ÷ v
  • Magnification, image height ÷ object height
  • Relative density, density ÷ density of water
  • pH
  • Angle in radians, arc ÷ radius

These are ratios, so the units cancel.

Non-SI units to convert

  • 1 litre = 10⁻³ m³
  • 1 km/h = 5/18 m s⁻¹
  • 1 calorie ≈ 4.18 J
  • 1 atm ≈ 1.013 × 10⁵ Pa
  • K = °C + 273
  • 1 tonne = 1000 kg
  • 1 atomic mass unit ≈ 1.66 × 10⁻²⁷ kg
